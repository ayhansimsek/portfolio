Who I Am

I'm an IT Support Engineer based in Melbourne with 5+ years of hands-on experience across school environments, enterprise retail, and international IT roles — spanning Australia, Ireland, Turkey, and the Philippines.

Currently at Source Central, I provide first and second-level support across multi-school environments, managing everything from Active Directory and Entra ID to MDM policies, network health, and cyber-safety initiatives. My background in Physics and Computer Science teaching shapes everything I do — I communicate complex technical concepts clearly, build knowledge bases that actually get used, and advise schools on improving their overall security posture.
