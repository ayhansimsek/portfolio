Background.

I am an IT support professional based in Melbourne with experience across school environments, enterprise retail and international IT roles in Australia, Ireland, Turkey and the Philippines.

Most recently at Source Central I provided first and second-level support across multiple schools, covering Active Directory and Entra ID, device management, network health and cyber safety. Before that, at Baby Bunting, I supported more than 70 stores across Australia and New Zealand and managed the lifecycle of over 500 Zebra devices.

I trained as a Physics and Computer Science teacher before moving into IT. Explaining technical ideas to people who are not technical has been part of every role I have had, and it is the reason I am now training to become a VET trainer.
